02 / The Crisis Slide 2 of 8

The Problem: Vulnerability to Monsoon and Lack of Guidance

Marginal farmers face regular crop failures and resource wastage due to critical gaps in weather advisories and diagnostic access.

Unpredictable Monsoons

Extreme climate shifts lead to unexpected localized dry-spells, ruining crop cycles without warning.

Hearsay-Based Decisions

Farmers plant based on habit or hearsay rather than soil health parameters, groundwater levels, or localized rainfall forecasts.

Wasted Resources & Financial Ruin

Wrong seed selection and improper irrigation timing lead to wasted input resources and massive financial debts.

04 / Component 1 Slide 4 of 8

Smart Crop Recommendation Engine

Leverages pre-mapped geographic telemetry to eliminate crop planting risks.

Geographic Lookup: Automatically matches districts (e.g. Kolar, Karnataka) with soil parameters.
Digital Soil Card: Calculates dynamic Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), soil pH, and moisture.
Dynamic Calibration: Farmers adjust values to run simulations and compare crop suitability indexes.
Recommended Varieties: Recommends specific crop types (e.g., Ragi, Tomato) matching target parameters.

05 / Component 2 Slide 5 of 8

Real-Time Advisory & Dry-Spell Alerts

Monitors fields dynamically to prevent dry-spell damage using localized alerts.

Ground Sensor Simulation: Tracks moisture, temperature, and groundwater parameters across village plots.
Automated Alerts: Instantly dispatches a warning when sensor levels fall below critical dry thresholds.
Indic SMS Dispatch: Sends clear warnings advising on irrigation times, fertilization adjustments, and water saving.
Voice Call Announcements: Multi-lingual Text-to-Speech call simulation reads alerts out loud for low-literacy users.

06 / Component 3 Slide 6 of 8

AI Crop Health Logger & RSK Connectivity

Enables rapid pest and pathogen diagnostic support by connecting farmers directly with regional agricultural officers.

Local File Uploader: Farmers pick leaf images via the file explorer or input voice memo queries.
Diagnostic AI Matching: Identifies crop pathogens (Rice Blast, Cotton Curl, Early Blight) with confidence metrics.
Instant Solutions: Lists immediate symptoms, organic prevention methods, and chemical treatment guidelines.
Rythu Seva Kendra dispatch: Automatically files support tickets, allowing RSK experts to review and follow up.
